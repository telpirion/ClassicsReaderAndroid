# Privacy Policy for the Latin Reader and Greek Reader app

The Latin Reader app for Android (v2.0, version code 20) and
the Greek Reader app (v1.0, version code 1) do not store any
personally-identifiable information (PII) from users. They do not collect any
sensitive user data. The apps also do not share any data with other apps or
services.

The apps do store information about user preferences when using the app.
Specifically, they store information about the user's preferred text size,
number of lines of text to display of a text, and whether the user prefers
to use arrow buttons for moving forward and backward in the text.

The apps also store the user's reading position in texts.
