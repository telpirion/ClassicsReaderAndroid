# Privacy Policy for Latin Reader and Greek Reader

Last Updated: May 29, 2026
Latin Reader version: 22
Greek Reader version: 1

Eric Michael Schmidt ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Latin Reader and Greek Reader mobile
applications (the "App(s)") available on the Google Play Store.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding
data collection and usage for the App.

1. No Data Collection or Storage
   The App is designed to function entirely as an offline reader and educational tool.

Personal Data: We do not collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information (such as
your name, email address, phone number, or precise location).

Usage Data: We do not track, log, or store any information regarding how you use the App, your
reading history, or your device performance.

2. No Data Sharing
   Because we do not collect any data from you or your device, we do not—and cannot—share, sell,
   rent, or trade any user data with third parties, advertisers, or external services.

3. No Advertising
   The App is 100% ad-free. We do not integrate any third-party advertising networks, software
   development kits (SDKs), or tracking pixels.

4. Children’s Privacy
   The App is intended for students and learners of all ages. Because the App does not collect any
   personal information whatsoever, it complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection
   Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding minors.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
   We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with
   an updated "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for
   any changes.

6. Contact Us
   If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact
   us by logging an issue on this GitHub repository.
